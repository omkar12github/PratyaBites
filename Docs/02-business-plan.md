# 🍔 PratyaBites Business Plan

> **Pratya's Promise, Every Bite.**

---

# 1. Executive Summary

PratyaBites is a Pure Vegetarian Cloud Kitchen that delivers fresh, hygienic, and affordable vegetarian fast food through its own online ordering platform.

The business focuses on quality food, quick delivery, and an excellent customer experience.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Many customers face issues such as:

- Unhygienic food
- Long delivery times
- High prices
- Poor customer service
- Limited vegetarian options

PratyaBites aims to solve these problems.

---

# 3. Solution

PratyaBites offers:

- 100% Pure Vegetarian food
- Fresh ingredients
- Hygienic preparation
- Fast delivery
- Affordable pricing
- Easy online ordering

---

# 4. Target Market

Primary Customers

- College Students
- Working Professionals
- Families

Secondary Customers

- Offices
- Small Events
- Corporate Meetings

---

# 5. Competitive Advantage

PratyaBites stands out by offering:

- Pure vegetarian menu
- Fast food with consistent quality
- Direct website ordering
- Lower prices by reducing dependency on third-party platforms
- Customer-focused service

---

# 6. Revenue Model

Revenue Sources

- Food Orders
- Combo Meals
- Party Orders
- Corporate Orders

Future Revenue

- Subscription Meals
- Franchise Fees
- Merchandise

---

# 7. Marketing Strategy

Online

- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp
- Google Business Profile

Offline

- Flyers
- Local Events
- Referral Programs

---

# 8. Business Goals

Short-Term

- Launch website
- Serve first 100 customers
- Build customer trust

Long-Term

- Open multiple cloud kitchens
- Launch Android and iOS apps
- Expand to new cities

---

# 9. SWOT Analysis

## Strengths

- Pure Vegetarian
- Modern Technology
- Affordable Pricing

## Weaknesses

- New Brand
- Limited Initial Budget

## Opportunities

- Growing online food delivery market
- Rising demand for vegetarian food

## Threats

- Competition
- Increasing ingredient costs

---

# 10. Conclusion

PratyaBites aims to become a trusted vegetarian food brand by combining excellent food quality, technology, and customer service.
